## Baseline evidence, edits, and gaps

Research anchors: the [five layers](../../../newsite/research/work-rows-and-case-framing/five-layers-of-a-case.md) and [decision-led narrative](../../../newsite/research/work-rows-and-case-framing/decisions-not-ceremony.md) are the report’s synthesis, not a measured causal recipe. [Ownership](../../../newsite/research/work-rows-and-case-framing/role-team-and-ownership.md), [visible craft](../../../newsite/research/seniority/craft-remains-visible.md), and [honest status](../../../newsite/research/proof-and-metrics/say-when-there-is-no-result.md) support the criteria. The weights, thresholds, and numerical judgments here are our editorial decisions. Presentation polish alone cannot create missing project evidence.

### Classroom

- Evidence: the hero identifies the teacher’s unanswered “who?” question. “Start with urgency” connects ten teacher sessions to grouping; “Making it work” names API IDs, the shared object-model workshop, and the deferred performance summary. Explicit personal actions plus named partner functions earn W4/J4.
- Preserve: sample-student label, original-count detail, research population, API constraint, and the distinction between hub adoption and student learning. These make E3/R3 credible without revealing confidential numbers.
- Required edits: make the shipped cut versus alternatives quicker to scan; add local next-case navigation in Work order. Existing content is already concise; do not manufacture a larger rewrite merely to increase its score.
- Gaps: confidential exact adoption figures are not a blocker. Source-reported qualitative/analytics claims need provenance retained; no independently verified learning impact exists.

### Indeed Job Refresh

- Evidence: unsolicited employer comments, the separate duplicate-reduction initiative, the false-duplicate risk, and the substantial-update requirement establish four connected decisions. The author distinguishes IA/flow/messaging/prototypes from wider-team delivery (W4/J4).
- Preserve: freemium exploration is research context; applicant-management concepts were paused for hiring-bias concerns. Trial → Sponsored Jobs is a delivery account, not evidence of revenue lift.
- Required edits: replace I/D/E/A labels with decisions; remove repeated explanation of meaningful updates across “Design Process” and “Action”; retain the employer journey and refresh eligibility surface. Make qualitative employer feedback visible alongside the delivery limit.
- Gaps: no quantified duplicate reduction, paid adoption, or revenue attribution. These can remain explicitly unavailable; they do not prevent a sound case.

### Curbside Pickup

- Evidence: field observations reveal switching pickup methods; Wi-Fi/login restrictions explain why app adoption alone cannot close the gap. Email ETA/check-in and app acknowledgment/recovery are complementary. The proposal names the app-download incentive tradeoff (J4).
- Preserve: store/customer perspectives and all three notification states. Reported 60-day/50-store outcomes carry stronger context than a generic “improved experience.”
- Required edits: consolidate field setup and decision rationale; replace ceremony labels; put precise author contribution and partners near the top. Keep numeric comparisons with their population and retrospective caveat.
- Gaps: [source evidence](../knowledge/evidence/curbside-pickup.md) redacts failure/longer-than-expected percentages and includes an unfinished old journey. Omit those figures and do not pass a reconstructed journey off as original research. No broader rollout can be claimed from pilot results.

### Price Adjustments

- Evidence: 12-store discovery, four form factors, multitasking, anchored fly-out selection, familiar “markdown” terminology, >$50 manager approval, store budgets, and SKU allocation connect research to an inspectable interaction (J4/C4).
- Preserve: apply/update/remove plus item/order paths, visible price calculation, and the working demonstration. Directional 30/60/90-day trends and 50-store pilot → rollout are separate kinds of evidence.
- Required edits: reorganize around retaining context, terminology, and business rules; remove process labels. Keep a static explanation of the demo for readers without JavaScript.
- Gaps: [source evidence](../knowledge/evidence/price-adjustments.md) has no numeric SUS score, transaction-time improvement, shrink reduction, or SOP magnitude. Do not invent them. Historical manager handoff should remain explicit; an illustrative demo must not imply it executes a real approval.

### Green Loom

- Evidence: Product/Variant/Listing/Inventory/Lab Result boundaries, source-derived potency, certificate expiry, guided-first versus direct-returning tasks, and neutral schema versus local profile show concrete system judgment. Original September wireframes and working sample stock controls earn C4.
- Preserve: original-versus-generated labels, sample data, separate schema/application names, and clear development status. The publication controls are implemented; retailer adoption and operator validation remain unestablished.
- Required edits: lead with the operator’s blocked publication/stock task, consolidate repeated model explanations, make alternatives and maintenance cost inspectable, and keep original wireframes more prominent than conceptual illustrations. Label applicable legal material as the project’s design context, avoiding a claim of comprehensive compliance.
- Source reconciliation: [October 2 direct author confirmation](/Users/aaron/Documents/ChatGPT/portfolio/green-loom-story-notes.md) supersedes September design-only status: the complete catalog includes working COA import and automatic publication blocks for missing/expired evidence. Preserve those completed capabilities without promoting them to retailer deployment.
- Gaps: deployment and task validation are absent. Record the next operator test rather than implying that internal implementation proves ease of use. October 2 regulatory checks are dated design context; currentness verification remains a separate factual check for any present-tense rule retained.

### UX MEOW

- Evidence: the author co-founded the program, wrote mentor training, led workshops, sourced projects, and modeled check-ins. Six teams growing to 16, returning mentors training others, and three full-time UX transitions are attributable to the shared program (W4).
- Preserve: exact responsibility boundaries, remote adaptation in 2020, and the distinction between field-tested explorations and production launches.
- Required edits: structure around the mentoring intervention and how it continued beyond one founder. Put a readable reconstructed training prompt/program structure ahead of generic illustrations (C2). Explain guidance versus autonomy as a decision without inventing participant reactions.
- Gaps: no original board, training sheet, or participant evaluation appears in this baseline. Reconstructed prompts are useful but cannot become verbatim participant quotations or evidence of measured leadership improvement. An actual training artifact would strengthen craft later; not a blocker to an honestly labeled account.

### Amazon A-to-z First Claim

- Evidence: analysis of 250 support contacts, seller interviews, tenets, the four email jobs, and advocating coverage with legal/finance/leadership provide traceable discovery and influence (W4). The under-$50 boundary is explicit.
- Preserve: the account-metric explanation and practical prevention steps; distinguish author advocacy/design from policy/budget authority. Reconstructed readable email avoids disclosing names in the original slide.
- Required edits: decision headings should explain learning versus discipline and coverage as a policy/communication tradeoff; replace ceremony labels. Keep the four email jobs inspectable and all program-level limitations together.
- Gaps: [source evidence](../knowledge/evidence/a-to-z-first-claim.md) calls retrospective results unverified and notes missing collaborator attribution. Keep 88% tied to email openers and 14% tied to related support calls at 60 days after U.S. launch; the 30% YoY trend is not isolated email causality. Do not promote deck figures to independently verified facts.

### Design Dash

- Evidence: object guides, fork/promote boundary, risk-scaled review, environment adapters, and portable linked Markdown records show deliberate choices (J4). The availability rule → wireframe → technical recheck sequence is inspectable.
- Preserve: the tool-library scenario is fictional; the method and its required gates are not user-research findings. Review cost and link-maintenance tradeoffs help avoid selling ceremony as proof.
- Required edits: move actual method artifacts/contract evidence ahead of large fictional illustrations; organize around portability, preserved reasoning, and proportionate review. State public/development status and the next validation exercise precisely.
- Gaps: usefulness to other teams and adoption are not established (E2). A transparent comparison task can be planned, but its results cannot be pre-written. Stronger evidence requires real reuse rather than another fictional diagram.

### Many Hats

- Evidence: independent critic separated from delivery; roles/skills/knowledge/work separation; scoped project corrections; the Library Holds critique removes branch transfers/preferences/incentives. These are testable contract choices (J4).
- Preserve: human accountability, citable objections, public cloneable v0.1, and the explicit no-adoption/no-field-study statement. A fictional trace proves inspectability, not real-world quality improvement.
- Required edits: lead with the smallest concrete proposal → challenge → reduced scope → retained decision example; shorten overlap between “engagement” and “action”; distinguish original implementation from simulated team outcomes.
- Gaps: no comparative effectiveness or external adoption evidence (E2). The baseline disclosure is empty because its detail paragraph sits outside the Pug disclosure block; migrate into a real native disclosure or visible paragraph. The case can honestly ship below target rather than invent validation.

### Shapes

- Evidence: shared identity versus representation, catalog/cart-price boundaries, documentation versus enforceable standards, contributor review, and first-authoring-path scope all expose concrete alternatives/maintenance cost (W4/J4). DOM-rendered Product record and unavailable-card states provide unusually substantial system/interface proof (C4).
- Preserve: six reference types, the Product→Product Card→availability→action→flow example, in-development fact, and proposed understanding/dependency/maintenance tasks. Do not present mock workspace elements as live controls.
- Required edits: make the current status and absence of measured validation explicit in the outcome, clarify retail example provenance, add a genuine next-case loop in Work order, and retain a reflection without repeating the opening thesis.
- Gaps: no completed designer/engineer comparison or upkeep study (E2). This is an honest development framework; proposed measures are not results. Dense tables require responsive and enlarged-text review before readiness.
