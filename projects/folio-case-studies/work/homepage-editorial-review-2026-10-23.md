One strong image and roughly two sentences per example; keep a supported consequence with each. No process galleries. Finish with “Explore all work.”

A brief credibility line can sit under the opening so experience is apparent before the projects. The detailed career block should not interrupt the three examples.

- Each selected project proves a different capability and states Aaron's contribution before its link.

- Modeling appears once as a homepage idea; detailed demonstrations live deeper.
- Shipped professional work and independent exploration are distinguishable.
- Full chronology has a clear destination, without reproducing it on Home.
- Existing palette, typography, restraint and useful imagery remain.
