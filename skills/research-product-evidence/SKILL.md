# Outcome

Produce a traceable evidence synthesis that reduces a named product uncertainty and states what the evidence can and cannot support.

# Workflow

1. Convert the product decision into answerable research questions.
2. Rank assumptions by consequence, uncertainty, and cost of being wrong.
3. Choose the least burdensome method capable of testing the highest-risk assumption.
4. Define population, sampling, recruitment, tasks, measures, and analysis before collection when primary research is involved.
5. Gather sources with provenance, date, context, and direct relevance.
6. Separate observations, participant statements, source claims, analyst inferences, and recommendations.
7. Code evidence consistently and map findings to objects, scenarios, or assumptions.
8. Seek disconfirming and contradictory evidence.
9. State confidence and limits based on source quality, sample, recency, and transferability.
10. Update the evidence and assumption records without deleting prior states.

# Safety and integrity

Obtain appropriate consent and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive data. Do not fabricate participants, quotations, citations, sample sizes, or findings. Treat simulated populations as hypothesis generators only.

# Output and verification

Include the decision, questions, method, evidence table, synthesis, contradictions, confidence, limitations, implications, and next research step. Copy `templates/work/research-plan.md` and `templates/work/research-synthesis.md`. Record durable claims with `knowledge/evidence/_template.md`. Verify every factual claim is cited or labeled as inference and every recommendation maps to evidence or an explicit assumption.
