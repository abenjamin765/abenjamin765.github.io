# The first screen names the person in one or two sentences

**Claim:** In the homepage sample, the opening is name, role, and where they work now, in one or two sentences.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`). The report’s 12/12 “clear role in the home-page introduction” is a related count on a different sample.

## Evidence

**Observed (homepage notes, E1):** Featured and sampled homepages introduce the person in one or two sentences. Confidence is high for that nine-site sample.

**Observed (report):** Clear role or positioning in the home-page introduction was coded 12/12. The report’s reading of that count: a reviewer is not made to infer the designer’s professional identity.

**Recorded lines (homepage notes):**

- Dharmesh Nayak: “Hi there, I am Dharmesh.” Then: “A Senior Product Designer with 6 years of experience. Currently cooking a stealth AI product at Zomato, India’s largest food delivery platform.”
- Aditya Muralidhar: name, then “Product designer based in ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು.”
- Vaibhav Verma: eyebrow “Senior product designer,” then “Hi, I’m Vaibhav. 6 years of end-to-end product design across FinTech, B2C and SaaS.”
- Niklas Peterson: name, “product designer and design engineer,” and “Most recently, I was a Staff Product Designer at LottieFiles.”
- Shahriyar Mohammadi: “Hey there, I’m Shahi a product designer based in London. Currently, staff product designer at Deel.”
- Yinyee Ho: “Hey, it’s Yinyee,” multidisciplinary designer in product and content.
- Rashmi Saini: “Hi I am Rashmi, I choreograph pixels for a living,” plus role, city, and 10+ years.
- Rishabh Yadav: the recorded lead is the claim “I grow design teams that move revenue,” not a job-title sentence in the notes’ excerpt.

**Observed limit:** Eemon Roy’s extracted hero was “Hi i’m Eemon a Product Designer at Microsoft and here’s everything i know about.” The homepage notes say the sentence is visibly unfinished; the ending was likely animated and was not recorded.

## Implication for this portfolio

The intro panel can add name, role, and current or most recent context to the existing “Hello.” The report’s 12/12 count supports “say the role,” not a specific sentence length beyond the homepage notes’ one or two sentences.

## Limit

Eemon’s missing ending means one of nine heroes was not fully captured. Rishabh’s excerpt shows that a leadership site may lead with the claim rather than “I am a senior product designer.”
