# Company names are a line, not the hero

**Claim:** The homepage notes allow company names in one line on the intro. They do not show a logo wall as the opener of the product-design homepages.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Current or most recent employer is inside the intro sentence for several sites (Zomato, Microsoft, LottieFiles, Deel). Joan Li is not in this sample; the report separately says her homepage pairs enterprise positioning with named products (Chrome, Azure AI, ViewSonic).

**Observed:** None of the product-design homepages in the homepage-notes sample open with a process diagram, a tool logo row, or a paragraph résumé.

**Inferred (homepage notes):** Company names can sit in one line on this site’s intro. A tool logo row should not.

## Implication for this portfolio

If employers appear in Intro, keep them to a line. The work row is where company and year repeat per project, in the pattern the notes describe for Dharmesh.

## Limit

The Wall’s own homepage has a logo row under its marketing headline. That is the catalog, not the featured designers’ heroes. See `../method-and-limits/wall-platform-claims.md`.
