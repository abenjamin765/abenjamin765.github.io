# The hero is a hiring thesis only as a reading

**Claim:** The report interprets a specific hero as a hiring thesis. That is an inference, not a test with hiring managers.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Vaibhav’s opening is “A designer who moves numbers,” followed by cases with business metrics. Dharmesh states senior product designer, six years, a direct email, then shipped work. Rishabh opens with team and revenue. Niklas opens at product design, design engineering, and code.

**Inferred (report):** The hero is functioning as a hiring thesis because the sentence could not belong to any designer.

## Implication for this portfolio

Write the intro claim so a reader can tell the kind of work, which is also what the homepage notes ask. Do not add “hiring thesis” as a label on the page, and do not treat a sharper sentence as evidence someone will be hired.
