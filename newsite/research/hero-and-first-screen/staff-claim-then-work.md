# Staff examples still use claim, then work

**Claim:** The homepage notes infer that a staff or principal product portfolio can use the same skeleton as the six-year consumer sites, and that the claim should name judgment and shipping because that is what those staff examples say.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Niklas Peterson: “Bringing digital products to life with pixels and code,” then staff product designer at LottieFiles, then “Selected product design case studies.” Shahriyar Mohammadi: staff product designer at Deel; the meta description recorded is “Ten years in product design, leveraging AI to shorten build cycles and create growth through insight and decisive iteration.” That homepage is described as a studio index (notes, writing, “work with me”) more than a case list.

**Inferred (E10):** Same skeleton as Dharmesh and Vaibhav: claim, then work. The claim names how they work: pixels and code, systems, decisive iteration, making a complicated surface understandable. They do not spend the hero on a biography. Confidence for E10 is medium.

**Inferred (synthesis):** Earlier-career consumer portfolios in the notes say things like “I design clear, intentional products” or “I move numbers.” Seniority shows up in the claim, not in a longer hero.

## Disagreement

Shahi’s live homepage does not follow “claim, then a case list.” The notes say that themselves, and later say the product-design features spend the homepage on shipped projects, while Shahi and Rishabh spend it on notes, leadership, and range. E10’s “same skeleton” fits Niklas more cleanly than Shahi.

The report’s staff and principal chapters ask for different proof inside the case (leverage, standards, multi-year influence). That is interior evidence, not a different homepage skeleton.

## Implication for this portfolio

If this intro is for an individual-contributor product portfolio, Niklas is the staff homepage in the notes that still leads into cases. Shahi is not the template for the work panel.

## Limit

Two staff-labeled homepages in the notes, one of which is a studio index. The directory card called Shahi senior; the live page said staff. Not a census of staff portfolios.
