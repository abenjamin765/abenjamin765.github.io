# Metric types the report actually attaches to people

**Claim:** The report’s metric table names kinds of evidence and, in most rows, a person. Unattached examples stay unattached.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`). Homepage notes repeat only Vaibhav’s 18% and £29.7M lines.

## Evidence

**Observed (report’s table):**

| Kind | What the report attributes |
| --- | --- |
| Conversion | Rishabh: onboarding 61.7% → 82.4% |
| Funnel behavior | Vaibhav: detail-to-booking 2.79% → 3.30%, about 18% lift |
| Revenue / market | Vaibhav: £29.7M annual transaction-based revenue and 4% market-share increase on a featured project |
| GMV | Aditya: ₹14L GMV during the first 17 days of one selector instance |

| Efficiency | Michelle: tested flow from 3:10 to 1:45 |
| Reach / scale | Eemon: Researcher Agent reported at 2M weekly users |
| Operational quality | Rishabh: design issues falling from 22% to 10% of QA bugs after the system landed |
| Discovery | Niklas: +316% monthly search volume and +35% monthly file views |

**Also observed:** Vaibhav’s pilot, 60% of 50 long-stuck cars booked within a week, with the caveats in `baseline-unit-interpretation.md`. Manolo’s Cornell case, as described later: hundreds of content items, more than 30 participants in tree testing, dozens of visual explorations.

**Not attributed:** The executive summary lists “171 survey responses + 12 interviews” as an example of specificity. The report does not name whose study that was in that sentence. Do not assign it.

**Homepage notes only:** Yinyee, “40+ early-access Marketplace developers,” as a scope line. Vaibhav card text “18% improvement in the booking funnel” and “£29.7M gross annual transaction-based revenue,” which matches the report’s funnel and revenue rows closely enough to be the same claims, worded differently.

## Implication for this portfolio

Use the table as a menu of evidence types, not as numbers to echo. Usability time, quality, and “not launched yet” are in the sample alongside revenue.

## Limit

Self-reported. Joan’s usability results are described, not quantified, in the sources we have.
