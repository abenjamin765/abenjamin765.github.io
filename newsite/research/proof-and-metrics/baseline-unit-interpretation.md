# A metric needs a baseline, a unit, and an interpretation

**Claim:** The report says the best portfolio metrics establish a baseline, an intervention, a unit, and an interpretation.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred:** Decorative numerals are not the pattern. P0: audit every numerical claim until baseline, unit, source or context, and attribution are defensible.

**Observed caution (Vaibhav, as the report tells it):** 60% of 50 long-stuck cars received bookings within a week. Buyer feedback is labeled qualitative, not a controlled experiment. The video concept did not scale.

**Inferred pitfall:** “Fake precision” weakens trust when causality cannot be defended. State what is measured, inferred, or merely directional.

## Implication for this portfolio

If the work row says “Shipped in six weeks” or “50-store pilot,” those phrases came from the homepage notes as existing hooks. They still need the same audit before they are published as results. This library did not re-check them.

## Limit

The report did not verify the underlying analytics. See `../method-and-limits/metrics-are-self-reported.md`.
