# Proof sits on the work item

**Claim:** The homepage notes say proof shows up as a shipped constraint or a number on the work item, not as a skill cloud in the hero.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (E5):** Medium confidence. Named: Dharmesh, Vaibhav, Yinyee, Niklas. Vaibhav’s cards lead with outcomes including “18% improvement in the booking funnel” and “£29.7M gross annual transaction-based revenue.” Yinyee includes a scope line for 40+ early-access Marketplace developers. Dharmesh’s recorded sentences state the constraint or the change without a percentage.

**Inferred:** Numbers help when attached to the row. They are not required for the row to work.

## Implication for this portfolio

Put the outcome on the work row. Do not move a metric stack into the intro in place of the claim.

## Limit

E5 is medium confidence. The report’s 10/12 count is “somewhere in prominent work or a reviewed case,” which is a wider net than “on the homepage row.” See `quantification-is-common.md`.
