# Say when there is no result to claim

**Claim:** The report says restraint about missing results increases credibility, and that business attribution should not be manufactured because metrics look good on a portfolio.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Rishabh’s DayUp product had not launched when the page was reviewed. He says there are no post-launch results to claim and lists six outcomes the team intends to measure. The report does not list those six outcomes by name.

**Inferred:** Do not manufacture business attribution. Entry-level metrics can legitimately be task success, completion time, SEQ or SUS-type measures, errors, comprehension, prototype conversion, accessibility issues resolved, adoption of a live side project, or test results. The report says revenue is not required at entry. It also says an entry portfolio’s job is not to impersonate a senior designer with invented revenue attribution.

## Implication for this portfolio

If a story has no customer result, say so and say what would be measured. Do not borrow a revenue figure from the sample to sound senior.

## Limit

“Increases credibility” is the report’s inference from Rishabh’s page and from Vaibhav’s caveats. It is not a trust experiment.
