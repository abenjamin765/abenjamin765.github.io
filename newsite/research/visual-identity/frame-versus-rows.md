# Identity frames the page; work rows stay plain

**Claim:** The homepage notes say personality is carried by voice, color, and a small illustration or motif, while the work rows stay plain.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (E6):** Confidence medium. Named sources: Eemon, Dharmesh, Yinyee, Aditya.

**Inferred:** Visual identity is the other half of what the curators praise. Eemon is featured for a playful system wrapped around Microsoft platform work. Aditya is featured for a clean system that stays personal. The identity is the frame. The rows inside it stay scannable.

**Inferred for this site:** Put personality in the yellow field, the portrait, and the voice of the claim. Keep the work rows typographically quiet.

## Disagreement

The Metro × Apple report puts product identity in the content layer and treats navigation chrome as native controls (`../design-system/content-plane-versus-control-plane.md`). These homepage notes put identity in the intro frame and keep the rows plain. Both can be true of different artifacts: the notes describe featured portfolios; the spec describes a cross-platform system. Neither source measured which reading a design director prefers on this page.

## Implication for this portfolio

Intro holds the identity. Work does not restyle each row into a poster. The yellow field and portrait are the notes’ description of the newsite intro, not something this library re-screenshotted.

## Limit

E6 is medium confidence. The report agrees that elaborate logos are not the differentiator, and it separately says custom interaction is optional. See `../interaction-and-tools/bespoke-interaction-is-optional.md`.
