# Minimal and strong identity are both in the source

**Claim:** The homepage notes record a real tension: the largest category is minimalist, and the September blurbs also reward playful and metric-heavy sites.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Minimalist 850+ is the largest printed bucket. September blurbs also praise playful identity and business-impact framing. Vaibhav is metric-heavy. Eemon is playful. Dharmesh is concise.

**Inferred (homepage notes):** “Be minimal” and “have a strong identity” are both present. They are not the same instruction. The featured set includes more than one of those looks.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not collapse this into “make it minimal” or “make it loud.” The notes’ split is identity in the frame, scannable rows inside it.

## Limit

The contradiction is about curator signals, not about which look hired anyone.
