# There is no winning visual template

**Claim:** The report’s central finding includes this negative result: there is no winning visual template.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`); Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred (report):** No winning visual template, fixed project count, or mandatory case-study length. Copying the visual treatment of a “winning” portfolio is the wrong lesson. Eemon’s playful Microsoft story, Michelle’s accessibility and design-engineering proposition, Kat’s long-horizon leadership, and Rishabh’s design-management operating system look different because they solve different hiring problems. The transferable unit the report names is the evidence architecture, not the aesthetic.

**Observed (homepage notes):** The featured set includes more than one look. The blurbs talk about clarity and identity, not about which category won.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not restyle Intro or Work to match a featured site’s theme. The notes already say palette, type, and motion are open.

## Limit

“No winning template” means the sample did not converge on one look. It does not mean visual identity is irrelevant. The blurbs praise identity and clarity together.
