**Claim:** The report codes highly evident bespoke interaction at 4/12 and treats it as an optional amplifier after clarity.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** 4/12. The four descriptions are Pranshu, Eemon, Vaibhav, and Rishabh, summarized in `../what-not-to-copy/interaction-must-not-gate-evidence.md`. The report says many equally credible winners use straightforward navigation.

A waving portrait or a yellow field, as the homepage notes describe the intro, is identity, not the bespoke-mechanic category. The report does not evaluate this site. Do not add a second interactive system to prove the portfolio is “interactive” in the Wall’s catalog sense.
