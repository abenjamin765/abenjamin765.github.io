# Distinctiveness is the proposition, not a logo

**Claim:** The report says the sampled winners are distinctive because of a concise professional proposition, not because each has an elaborate logo.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed lines the report uses:** “moves numbers” (Vaibhav); design-plus-code (Niklas); accessible products that actually ship (Michelle, via “I ship the front-end” and the accessibility detail); “systems over heroics” and revenue or team leverage (Rishabh); “clarity beats cleverness” (Manolo).

**Inferred:** Personal branding in the coding scheme was role clarity, a memorable point of view, and visual or verbal differentiation. The homepage notes make a compatible observation: personality is voice, color, and a small motif, while rows stay plain.

## Implication for this portfolio

Spend the intro on one proposition. Do not add a logo system to match this finding. The finding is that the logo was not the pattern.

## Limit

“Less because each has an elaborate logo” is the report’s comparison. It does not inventory logos.
