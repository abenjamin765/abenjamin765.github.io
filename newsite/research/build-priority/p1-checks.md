# Report checklist: P1

**Claim:** The report’s P1 checks cover scan summaries, artifact cuts, a failure or trade-off, image sequence, a résumé link, reflow, keyboard, motion, and media performance.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Prescribed (report), done when:**

- Each case can be understood from an outcome summary without reading the full page.
- Every shown artifact changes or proves something in the story.
- At least senior-level cases include one failure, trade-off, or reflection.
- Product images explain before/after, states, iterations, or final behavior.
- A résumé or CV is available, even though only half the report’s sample exposed one.
- No horizontal content failure at narrow widths or zoom.
- Every actionable element works without a mouse, and focus is visible.
- Motion is controllable and does not gate content.
- Hero videos or images do not make the evidence painfully slow to reach.

The report says the accessibility items are baseline tests, not aesthetic refinements, and cites W3C guidance on keyboard access, focus visibility, and reflow. That guidance is borrowed.

## Implication for this portfolio

P1 is how a work story and the whole page hold up after the rows exist. The résumé item matches the notes’ “résumé is a link,” with the sample-size caveat in `../navigation/resume-is-a-link.md`.

## Limit

“Half the sample” is the report’s 6/12 résumé code. The homepage notes do not use that fraction.
