# Report checklist: P0

**Claim:** The report’s P0 checks are about role, cases, ownership, headings, decisions, numbers, and contact. They are prescriptions, not observed frequencies.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Prescribed (report), done when:**

- One sentence describes what you should be hired to own next.
- Role and distinctive leverage are understandable in one viewport.
- 2–4 hero cases each prove a different hiring-relevant capability.
- Every case states role, team, and scope so a reviewer can separate the person from the team.
- Project headings communicate more than project or company names.
- Each case has 2–4 pivotal decisions, so the narrative does not depend on chronological process.
- Every numerical claim has a defensible baseline, unit, source or context, and attribution.
- An email or contact path is available without hunting.

## Disagreement

The homepage notes do not set “what you should be hired to own next,” and they do not set 2–4 cases. They do support a one-sentence claim, headings that name the situation, and a visible contact path. See `../hero-and-first-screen/one-claim-not-a-trait-list.md`, `../work-rows-and-case-framing/hero-cases-and-breadth.md`, and `../navigation/contact-path.md`.

## Implication for this portfolio

Use P0 as the report’s editing list for stories and the intro claim. Do not treat “hired to own next” as a fact this research established.

## Limit

Priority labels are the author’s. No stack-rank study sits behind P0 versus P1.
