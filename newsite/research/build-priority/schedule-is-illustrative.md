# The six-week schedule is illustrative

**Claim:** The report includes a build timeline of about six weeks and says that duration is an illustrative schedule, not a requirement.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** The report says a practical development cycle is approximately six weeks for a substantial rebuild, and that this is an illustrative schedule, not a claim that six weeks is required. The attached Gantt uses dates starting 2026-10-19 and phases labeled Evidence, Narrative, Experience, Validation, and Interview.

**Not placed as a plan:** The day-by-day Gantt is not copied into this library as this site’s schedule. The phase order is the part that can be looked up: inventory the thesis and projects; gather metrics, artifacts, permissions, and testimonials; rewrite flagship cases around decisions and outcomes; cut duplication; set homepage hierarchy; then visual system, responsive implementation, accessibility, and performance; then a short reviewer pass; interview presentation in parallel later.

## Disagreement

The Metro × Apple report has a second Gantt that also starts on 2026-10-19 and runs to a 2027-01-17 milestone (`../design-system/rollout-is-proposed.md`). That one is a proposed design-system rollout, and the report says the dates are not external facts. Same calendar anchor, different plan. Do not merge the two diagrams.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not adopt 19 October 2026, a six-week clock, or the 90-day design-system rollout. The homepage notes do not propose a schedule.

## Limit

The Gantt’s task durations are the author’s illustration. They are not estimates for `newsite/`.
