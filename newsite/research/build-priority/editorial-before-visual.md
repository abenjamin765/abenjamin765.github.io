**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred:** A weak case in an exquisite shell remains weak. A clearly evidenced case can survive a relatively simple shell. The report’s sequence is: clarify the hiring thesis, curate the evidence, expose the decisions, substantiate the outcomes, establish ownership, make the story scannable, show the craft, remove friction, then add personality.

**Disagreement with the homepage notes:** Those notes treat visual identity as half of what the September blurbs praise, and they treat the intro’s hello, color, and portrait as the identity frame. They do not say to finish all editorial work before any visual identity exists. The report’s sequence puts personality last. The notes put identity on the first screen and keep the rows quiet. A designer can hold both: the frame can be specific, and interaction or decoration should not outrun the work rows.
