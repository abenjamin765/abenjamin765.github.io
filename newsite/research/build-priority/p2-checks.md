# Report checklist: P2

**Claim:** The report puts testimonials, side work, bespoke interaction, an interview deck, and mock interviews after the evidence path.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Prescribed (report), done when:**

- Testimonials describe collaboration or influence, not vague praise.
- Side projects or writing reinforce the professional thesis rather than dilute it.
- Bespoke interaction is added only after conventional navigation is excellent.
- The best case also exists as a separate presentation.
- The author can explain every number, decision, and conflict without the website.

## Implication for this portfolio

None of these are required to resolve the intro and work-row question in the homepage notes. Interaction and the interview deck are explicitly later.

## Limit

P2 is a sequence recommendation. The sample includes people who already have bespoke interaction (4/12). The report does not say those sites were wrong; it says interaction is not the prerequisite.
