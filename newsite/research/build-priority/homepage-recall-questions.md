# Ask what the homepage communicated

**Claim:** The report’s suggested critique is a short recall test, not a taste poll.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Prescribed:** After a brief look at the homepage, ask: What level do you think I am? What kind of problems do I solve? Which project would you open? What evidence do you remember? What is my strongest capability?

**Inferred:** That turns portfolio critique into something closer to a usability study. The report does not report running this test on the twelve sites.

## Implication for this portfolio

These five questions are a way to review Intro plus the work rows later. They are not results. “What level” will return an impression, not a hire decision.
