# Do not copy a process diagram into the hero or the row

**Claim:** A standard UX activity sequence is what the report calls process theater, and the homepage notes say the product-design homepages do not open with a process diagram.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`); Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (homepage notes):** None of the product-design homepages in that sample open with a process diagram, a tool logo row, or a paragraph résumé.

**Inferred (report):** Process theater makes every case look interchangeable. The better move is to organize around 2–4 consequential decisions. The weak sequence named is Research → Persona → Journey Map → Wireframes → UI.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not add a process diagram to Intro or to the work rows. Inside a story, show the decision the process changed.

## Limit

“2–4 decisions” is the report’s prescription. The homepage notes do not count decisions per case.
