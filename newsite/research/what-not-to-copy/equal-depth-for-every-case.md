# Do not give every case the same depth

**Claim:** The report lists “ten equally deep cases” as a failure mode because it signals an inability to curate.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred:** The better move is 2–4 hero cases and a compressed rest. Niklas exposing ten links is not, in the report, the same as ten equal long cases; the report says those staff stories can be exceptionally concise.

**Observed (homepage notes):** The notes do not set a maximum number of rows. They describe lists of different lengths, including Niklas’s selected cases and Yinyee’s four works.

## Implication for this portfolio

The work panel can list several rows. Equal long treatments for every row is the thing the report says not to copy. The homepage notes do not independently set the 2–4 number.

## Limit

“Ten” is the pitfall’s illustration. Niklas’s ten links are a different fact. Do not read the pitfall as “Niklas was wrong to show ten.”
