# Interaction must not gate the evidence

**Claim:** The report says custom interaction is neither necessary nor sufficient, and that it should not make the reviewer earn access to the work.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`); contrast in the homepage notes.

## Evidence

**Observed (report):** Highly evident bespoke interactive mechanics were coded 4/12. The report describes Pranshu’s site as unusually experiential: local weather, personalized visitor interaction, conversational onboarding, a sky and drink motif, galleries, and interactive side projects, around a history spanning Intuit, Tesla, and student work. Eemon provides an animation-pause affordance and playful personality. Vaibhav uses a 60-second introduction and DVD or television metaphors for design videos. Rishabh uses flip cards, a “60-second version,” and a design-leadership side quest. The report says many equally credible winners use straightforward navigation.

**Observed (homepage notes):** Rishabh’s work is a card game. Eemon’s hero sentence appeared to animate. Pranshu is not in the nine-site sample.

**Inferred (report):** Interaction should increase memorability after clarity is secured. The pitfall “interaction before usability” is a creative shell that slows access to work. The better move is a bypass or a straight path to projects. P1: motion is controllable and does not gate content. P2: add bespoke interaction only after the conventional path is excellent. Pranshu is the report’s example that a memorable world can be an asset when email, LinkedIn, and résumé remain available.

## Disagreement

The Metro × Apple report adds durations and a reduced-motion rule (`../design-system/motion-intents.md`). It agrees that decorative motion is not the point. It does not change the 4/12 count, and it does not choose an animation for this intro.

## Implication for this portfolio

Intro can have personality. The path to Work, About, and Contact should not depend on completing an animation or a game. The homepage notes already refuse to decide motion as a style. Reduced motion, if the intro moves, is the later spec’s requirement, not a Wall finding.

## Limit

4/12 is “highly evident,” not “eight sites have no interaction.” The report does not name the eight.
