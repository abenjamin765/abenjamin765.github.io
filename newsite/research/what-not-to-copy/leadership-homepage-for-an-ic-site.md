# A leadership homepage is a different job

**Claim:** The homepage notes say sites that lead with team, revenue, or growth are a contrast, not the template for an individual-contributor case portfolio.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`). The report treats some of the same people as strong leadership examples.

## Evidence

**Observed (E7):** The two leadership-framed sites lead with team, revenue, or growth rather than a product story. Confidence medium, n=2. Named: Rishabh, and Shahi’s meta description.

**Observed:** Rishabh: “I grow design teams that move revenue,” then a card game. The notes call this design-manager positioning, not an individual-contributor case portfolio. Shahi mixes notes, writing, and “work with me.”

**Inferred (homepage notes):** For an individual-contributor portfolio, the pattern that matches the featured product designers is the one that spends the homepage on shipped projects.

## Disagreement

The report includes Rishabh as one of the strongest leadership portfolios in its sample and builds a lead playbook from that kind of evidence. It also lists his homepage in the outcome-first archetype. That is not a retraction of the notes. It is a different question: how a design manager presents leadership, versus what an IC product homepage in the featured set does.

## Implication for this portfolio

If the intro is for an individual-contributor product designer, do not lead with team-growth or revenue as the hiring thesis. The report’s lead and principal chapters are available if a story is actually about that scope. They are not the homepage skeleton the notes recommend.

## Limit

n=2 in the notes. The report’s leadership chapters add Kat Angeles, who was not in the homepage pass.
