# A gallery without a decision is not the work list

**Claim:** The report says a gallery that shows taste without product judgment is a recurring failure.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred (pitfall):** “Gallery without reasoning” shows taste but not product judgment. The better move is to attach each visual to a problem, a choice, or an outcome.

**Related observation:** All 12 sites use visuals; the report’s distinction is whether the image has a job. See `../visual-identity/images-answer-a-question.md`.

## Implication for this portfolio

The work panel’s rows should say what changed. A strip of screens without that sentence matches the pitfall, not the homepage notes’ row.

## Limit

The report does not name a sampled site as a pure gallery. The pitfall is a pattern it warns against, not a headcount.
