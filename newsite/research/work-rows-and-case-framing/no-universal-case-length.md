# There is no universal case length

**Claim:** Both the report’s sample and the career guidance it cites refuse a single correct case length.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Structural depth runs from roughly four to six major blocks (Niklas’s LottieFiles story, Aditya’s category-discovery case) to Rishabh’s DayUp case in 13 numbered sections and Manolo’s extensive Cornell case. See `../method-and-limits/dynamic-pages-skew-counts.md`.

**Borrowed:** AIGA’s current career guidance, as the report summarizes it, says there is no single mandatory portfolio format, project count, or case-study length. Nielsen Norman Group emphasizes curation and scanability because hiring managers are time constrained.

**Inferred (report):** The level tables give approximate reading times (entry about 5–8 minutes, mid about 7–10, senior about 8–12, and variable after that). The report says these are synthesized targets, not hiring-industry laws, and that they favor scanability.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not pad a work story to hit a minute count. The homepage notes’ open question remains how much process a staff-level story shows before the decision and the outcome. The report’s staff chapter answers that for its own examples by compressing process, which is a different study.

## Limit

The minute ranges are not timed reads of the twelve sites. They are the report’s targets.
