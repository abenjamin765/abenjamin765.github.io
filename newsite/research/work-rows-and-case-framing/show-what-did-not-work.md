# Show something that did not work

**Claim:** The report says the strongest senior cases include a failure, a trade-off, or an unresolved issue, and that a perfect retrospective can read as less senior.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (report’s account):** Vaibhav’s video pilot was promising and did not scale, because production was too expensive and business priorities changed. He labels buyer feedback as qualitative rather than a controlled experiment. Aditya includes “What I would do differently.” Michelle describes a first version replaced after testing. Kat’s principal IA narrative includes partial progress and organizational interruption.

**Inferred:** Experienced reviewers know real product work contains failure, constraint, and incomplete outcomes. Sanitizing those away can make a case feel less senior. P1: at least senior-level cases demonstrate honest judgment. The pitfall name is “perfect retrospective.”

## Implication for this portfolio

A work story can include the cut, the version that lost, or the result that was not reached. The homepage notes do not require this on the row. The row can stay plain while the story holds the trade-off.

## Limit

“Can make a case feel less senior” is the report’s inference. It is not a reviewer study. The homepage sample did not test whether failure stories were present.
