# The repeated work-row unit

**Claim:** The homepage notes’ clearest row is company and year, a short name, and one sentence about what changed for a person. A metric can sit under the sentence. It is not required.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** Dharmesh’s rows, quoted in `../questions-as-headlines/row-readable-before-the-case.md`. Niklas omits the year. Vaibhav adds a metric. Aditya puts the problem in the title.

**Observed (E5):** Proof shows up as a shipped constraint or a number on the work item, not as a skill cloud in the hero. Confidence medium. Sources named: Dharmesh, Vaibhav, Yinyee, Niklas.

**Inferred:** Numbers help when they are attached to that row. Dharmesh is praised for concise storytelling and does not lead with percentages.

## Disagreement

The Metro × Apple report says a project summary may be a card, and also says not to card every row (`../design-system/cards-are-objects.md`). These notes describe a plain row, not a card. A card is allowed by the spec and not required by the homepage sample.

## Implication for this portfolio

Use that row on the Work panel. Keep the rows typographically quiet so they can be scanned. Personality stays in the intro. See `../visual-identity/frame-versus-rows.md`.

## Limit

“Clearest repeated unit” is the notes’ judgment, anchored on Dharmesh, not a count of how many of the nine used all three parts.
