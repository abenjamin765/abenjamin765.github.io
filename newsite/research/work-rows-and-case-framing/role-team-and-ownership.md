# Say what you owned

**Claim:** The report says the strongest cases distinguish “I” from “we,” so corporate work can be credited.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (as the report describes the pages):** Dharmesh names his PM, visual-design, data-science, and engineering partners. Joan names a two-person UX team. Michelle names a four-person product team and states which UX responsibility she owned. Rishabh names his three-designer team and describes when he delegated and when he personally took over a critical path.

**Inferred (report):** Unqualified “we” makes ownership impossible to evaluate. The better move is an explicit role, team, and contribution block. P0: reviewers can distinguish your contribution from the team’s.

**Inferred for entry, still the report:** Personal and team disclosure should be unusually explicit.

## Implication for this portfolio

Each work story that names a team should say what this designer owned. The homepage notes do not document team blocks, because they stayed on the homepage.

## Limit

These four team descriptions are the report’s examples. They are not a 12/12 count.
