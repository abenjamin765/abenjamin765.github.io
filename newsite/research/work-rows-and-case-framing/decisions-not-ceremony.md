# Organize the story around decisions

**Claim:** The report says process is present in strong cases, and the story is organized around decisions rather than ceremony.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (report’s reading of Eemon’s Researcher Agent case):** It begins with the behavioral shift from asking AI questions to delegating long-running work, not with “Empathize.” A UI requirement, show progress on the taskbar, is reframed as a trust problem. Many backend states are reduced to four user-facing states. An edge case changes the design. Scale is given as 2 million weekly users.

**Observed decision examples the report lists:** Eemon’s state reduction; Dharmesh’s scope reduction; Aditya’s choice to make the first category card media rich; Vaibhav’s pilot trade-off; Rishabh’s decision to prototype before PRDs.

**Inferred:** A stronger sequence than a process inventory is evidence, changed understanding, competing options, decision, consequence. P0: identify 2–4 pivotal decisions per case so the narrative does not depend on chronological process.

## Implication for this portfolio

A work story for this site should make the decision visible. The homepage notes do not confirm that pattern inside cases. They do say the work row should say what changed.

## Limit

“2–4 pivotal decisions” is a prescribed count. The case examples are illustrations, not a census of decisions per page.
