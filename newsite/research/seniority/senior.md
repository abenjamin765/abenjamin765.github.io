# Senior: show judgment under ambiguity

**Claim:** The report says a senior portfolio has to show bets under ambiguity, trade-offs, and more than one kind of metric when those metrics exist.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred targets:** 3–4 hero cases, about 8–12 minutes. Central question: can they make good bets under ambiguity and own outcomes? Evidence: product and business framing, trade-offs, systems and edge cases, instrumentation, mentorship.

**Inferred template:** Executive summary, user and business stakes, ownership, diagnosis, evidence, strategic options, decision logic, edge cases, cross-functional negotiation, ship and instrument, impact, what failed, next bet.

**Observed examples:** Dharmesh shows an ambitious idea and the decision to reduce scope. Vaibhav combines funnel research, interviews, interaction design, measurable lift, and a pilot that did not operationalize at scale.

**Inferred:** Multiple metric kinds where available: user, product or business, and quality or operational. A senior designer should also know when attribution is impossible. Formal direct reports are unnecessary for leadership evidence such as mentoring, influencing PM or engineering, or making another team’s work easier.

## Implication for this portfolio

Dharmesh and Vaibhav are already homepage evidence. This file adds the report’s interior reading. It does not reclassify this site as senior, and it does not require revenue on every row.

## Limit

“Normally show multiple kinds of metrics” is the report’s “should.” Dharmesh’s homepage, in the notes, does not lead with percentages.
