# Staff: show leverage across systems

**Claim:** The report says staff is an individual-contributor leverage story: cross-team or cross-system effect without relying on direct authority.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`); homepage skeleton in `newsite/research.md`.

## Evidence

**Inferred targets:** 3–5 hero cases, often shorter than expected, variable depth. Central question: can they create leverage across teams and systems without formal authority? Evidence: architecture, reusable systems, adoption, governance, technical partnership, influence.

**Inferred template:** Ecosystem problem, leverage thesis, direction or system architecture, one detailed decision, technical constraints, cross-team adoption, standards, rollout, reusable impact, organizational influence.

**Observed example:** Niklas Peterson’s LottieFiles case, as described: responsibility and partner teams, information architecture, interaction and motion, reusable patterns, rollout, design reviews, QA, final approval, and the system becoming a foundation for other designers. Metrics the report attaches: +316% monthly search volume and +35% monthly file views.

**Inferred artifacts:** Principles, system architecture, component or rule taxonomies, cross-product prototypes, technical explorations, adoption models, contribution guidelines, decision frameworks, governance, migration plans, instrumentation, quality mechanisms. The report says personas and journey maps are often not the key staff artifacts.

**Inferred warning:** The danger is abstraction, such as “I influenced five teams” without the decision mechanism. “Five teams” is the report’s example of a vague claim, not a result from the sample.

**Homepage notes:** Niklas’s homepage is still claim, then selected cases. See `../hero-and-first-screen/staff-claim-then-work.md`.

## Implication for this portfolio

This is the interior altitude closest to the question the homepage notes already ask. The homepage can stay claim-then-work. A staff story, if that is the scope, should show a reusable decision and what changed, not a process textbook and not a management homepage.

## Limit

Niklas is the chapter’s main example. The notes also include Shahi as a staff title on a studio-index homepage, which the report does not use. One staff homepage in the report’s sample is not a staff census.
