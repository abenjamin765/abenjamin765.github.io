# What the report’s comparison table changes by level

**Claim:** The report’s glance table is a synthesis of how proof is supposed to change with scope. It is not a count of the 12 sites.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred (report’s table):**

| Dimension | Entry | Mid | Senior | Lead | Staff | Principal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main proof | Fundamentals | Independent execution | Judgment and outcomes | Team and product leverage | Cross-team or system leverage | Organizational strategy |
| Research | Show method clearly | Choose methods appropriately | Synthesize weak or conflicting evidence | Guide team research priorities | Shape learning systems | Reframe strategic questions |
| Craft | High | High | High | Must remain visible | Representative depth | Selectively deep |
| Business framing | Basic | Explicit | Strong | Strong | Portfolio or system level | Strategic |
| Artifacts | Research, flows, iterations, tests | Decisions and implementation | Systems, trade-offs, data | Operating mechanisms and selected craft | Architecture, standards, adoption | Strategic models, standards, executive artifacts |
| Metrics | Usability and learning | Shipped behavior | User, business, and quality | Product and team | System, adoption, leverage | Product, portfolio, and organization |
| Leadership | Collaboration | Ownership | Mentoring and influence | Team direction | Influence without authority | Organization-level influence |
| Failure story | Learning | Iteration | Trade-off | Team or product correction | Adoption or strategy correction | Structural, long-horizon lesson |
| Personality | Potential and curiosity | Professional point of view | Defined specialty | Leadership philosophy | Technical or product thesis | Strategic worldview |

Narrative versions of each column are in `entry.md`, `mid.md`, `senior.md`, `lead.md`, `staff.md`, and `principal.md`.

## Implication for this portfolio

Use the staff column only as the report’s description of staff-scoped proof, and only together with the homepage notes’ warning that a leadership homepage is a different job. Do not print this table on the site.

## Limit

The report says these are synthesized targets, not hiring-industry laws. The homepage notes do not contain this table.
