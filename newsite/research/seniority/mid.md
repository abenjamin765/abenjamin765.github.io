# Mid: show independent shipping

**Claim:** The report says the mid-level portfolio should show ownership of a meaningful piece of a real product, not only that the author understands UX process.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred targets:** 3–4 hero cases, about 7–10 minutes. Central question: can they independently get useful work shipped? Evidence: constraints, cross-functional decisions, implementation, measurable validation.

**Inferred template:** Outcome snapshot, context and role and team, problem evidence, constraints, 2–3 major decisions, iteration, implementation, validation, metric, reflection.

**Observed examples:** Joan Li’s ViewSonic project, as described: user problems and company positioning, stakeholder regions, versions that failed for specific reasons, measurable usability differences, global implementation, research across ten countries, three design iterations. Aditya Muralidhar, described as a more compressed alternative: why a familiar category pattern needed a product-specific adaptation, reach or conversion, and a trade-off he would test next. Aditya is also a September feature with 2 years in the report’s excerpt table, so the report is using him as a compressed case, not only as a “mid” title.

**Inferred metrics:** Shipped behavior: conversion, activation, engagement, adoption, support volume, task efficiency, or funnel movement, rather than only prototype sentiment.
