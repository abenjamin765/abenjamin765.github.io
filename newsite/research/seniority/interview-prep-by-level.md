# Interview preparation in the report, by level

**Claim:** The report adds interview advice under each level. It is borrowed or prescriptive, and it is not homepage evidence.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Prescribed (report):**

- Entry: build a 12–15 minute presentation from one case rather than navigating the site live. Rehearse why each step changed a decision. NN/g is cited for converting the best case into a presentation and rehearsing behavioral answers.
- Mid: focus on why that method, why not the other concept, which constraint mattered, what engineering rejected, what data contradicted, and what another month would change.
- Senior: prepare one deep case and short answers for failures, disagreement, scope cuts, data conflicts, and trade-offs between user and commercial needs. STAR is called a useful minimum structure. NN/g is cited for examples of compromise, stakeholder work, and testing decisions.
- Lead: map the team around every major example: who owned what, whom you coached, what you decided, what you delegated, how you handled underperformance or disagreement, and what changed in the operating model.
- Staff: one presentation that explains a system through a concrete example, so “I influenced five teams” is not left abstract. “Five teams” is the report’s example of a vague line, not a sample result.
- Principal: stories about a strategy initially rejected, a cross-org disagreement, a decision that took years, a change in leadership’s framing, something killed, an initiative that outlived direct involvement, and a case where the desired outcome was not reached.

The shared point is in `../audience-and-hiring-context/interview-is-separate.md`: do not present by scrolling the website.
