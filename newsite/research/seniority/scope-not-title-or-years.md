# Level the portfolio by scope, not by a title

**Claim:** The report says seniority labels are not standardized, so its playbook uses scope signals rather than years.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** The Wall contains titles across varied experience levels. The report says people’s responsibilities were more informative than title alone.

**Inferred progression:** Entry proves method and potential. Mid proves independent delivery. Senior proves judgment and outcomes. Lead proves team leverage. Staff proves cross-system leverage. Principal proves durable strategic influence.

**Inferred:** The level tables are synthesized targets, not hiring-industry laws. They favor scanability. Borrowed support named: hiring managers often scan, and practitioner guidance recommends keeping case-study reading bounded.

## Implication for this portfolio

The homepage notes already ask which patterns fit a staff-level product portfolio. That is a scope question, not a request to print “Staff” in the intro. The HTML title is “Software Designer,” which this progression does not define.

## Limit

Do not convert the six labels into a promotion plan. The report says years of experience do not define level.
