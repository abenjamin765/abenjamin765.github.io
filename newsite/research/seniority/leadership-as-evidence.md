# Leadership has to be shown, not listed

**Claim:** The report says leadership becomes evidence when other people describe specific behaviors, or when the operating system is inspectable.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (Kat, as described):** Testimonials come from a VP or Head of UX, a UX director, a principal technical PM, and engineers. The report says they describe mentorship, design-review effectiveness, cross-functional trust, technical accommodation, and consensus-building. The case architecture supports that with a nine-year product arc. The report does not reproduce the testimonial quotations.

**Observed (Rishabh, as described):** Team-growth principles, design-review systems, mentoring, design-system adoption, delivery acceleration, and scope that changes over a career. Not a skill labeled “leadership.”

**Inferred:** P2 checklist: add testimonials where they prove senior behaviors. Quotes should describe collaboration or influence, not vague praise. Side projects and writing are also P2, and only when they reinforce the professional thesis.

## Implication for this portfolio

The About panel is the notes’ place for a shorter human block, not a testimonial wall. Testimonials are a report-level P2, and only if a real quote does the job the report describes. Do not draft praise.
