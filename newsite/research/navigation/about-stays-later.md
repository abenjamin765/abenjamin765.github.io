# About stays later and shorter

**Claim:** The homepage notes place About after the work, and keep it short. Leadership and studio homepages that lead with range are a different pattern.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** About is a later, shorter block in the product-design homepages the notes describe. Shahi’s page mixes selected notes, writing, and a “work with me” block and is called a studio index. Rishabh’s supporting lines are about teams, systems, and prototypes as a shared language.

**Inferred (homepage notes):** Let the intro panel stop at hello, portrait, name, role, and one claim. Experience, about, and a long biography are separate panels already.

## Implication for this portfolio

The About panel can exist. The notes say it should not be pulled up into Intro, and it should not become the homepage argument in place of Work.

## Limit

The notes did not transcribe About page copy. “Shorter” is their summary, not a measured word count.
