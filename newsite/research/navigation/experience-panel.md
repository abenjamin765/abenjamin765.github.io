# Experience is the extra destination

**Claim:** The homepage notes say Experience is not one of the destinations the sample repeats. If the panel stays, it should be the résumé, not a second telling of the work.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed in markup:** `newsite/index.html` has an Experience panel between Intro and Work. In the file read for this library, its content is the placeholder “Hello.”

**Inferred (homepage notes):** Sampled sites put experience, about, and a long biography later, and keep them short. If Experience stays, it should be the résumé, not a second version of the work stories.

**Observed (report, related):** Early/mid cases show research, flows, iterations, and testing more explicitly. Staff and principal examples compress artifact chronology. That is about case depth, not about a homepage section named Experience.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not fill Experience with the same stories as Work. The notes’ use for it is a short résumé. The report does not require a panel with this name.

## Limit

No sampled site in the notes is described as having a section titled Experience. This is an inference about this site’s existing panel, not an observed pattern.
