# Navigation labels stay short

**Claim:** In the homepage sample, navigation is short. Repeated labels are Work, About, and Contact or Resume.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (E4):** Confidence high for that sample. Recorded nav:

- Dharmesh Nayak: Work, About me, Contact, Resume.
- Aditya Muralidhar: Projects, About, Resume.
- Eemon Roy: Work, Sidequests, About.

The notes do not print a full nav list for every one of the nine. Vaibhav, Niklas, Yinyee, Shahi, Rashmi, and Rishabh are described through hero and work, not a complete menu transcript.

**Inferred (homepage notes):** Intro, Work, About, and Contact match the sample. Experience is the extra destination on this site.

## Implication for this portfolio

`newsite/index.html` already uses Intro, Experience, Work, About, and Contact. The notes support Intro, Work, About, and Contact. Experience is the one label the sample does not repeat. See `experience-panel.md`.

## Limit

E4 says “repeated labels,” not “these four and no others.” Eemon’s Sidequests is a recorded extra. The report does not publish a navigation-label frequency table.
