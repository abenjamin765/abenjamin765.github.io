# Contact is a visible path

**Claim:** A direct contact route is common in the report’s sample, and the homepage notes show contact as a short invitation, not a hunt.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`); Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (report):** Directly visible contact route, 10/12. The report’s reading: conversion from “interested” to “contact” is usually deliberately easy. P0 check: email or a contact path is available without hunting. Dharmesh’s homepage exposes email. Pranshu’s experiential site still has email, LinkedIn, and résumé.

**Observed (homepage notes):** Dharmesh has a “Get in touch” link in the intro block. Navigation includes Contact. Contact copy recorded: “Your turn. Say hello.” The notes say contact is an invitation in one line.

## Disagreement

The report’s 10/12 means two sites in that sample did not have a directly visible contact route. It does not name them. The homepage notes do not report a missing-contact count.

## Implication for this portfolio

The Contact panel should be reachable from the nav that is already in `newsite/index.html`. One line is enough, based on the notes. Neither source specifies the form fields.

## Limit

“Conversion” is the report’s word. Neither source measured whether people actually wrote.
