# A résumé is a link, not the argument

**Claim:** Both sources treat a résumé as useful and secondary. They do not agree on how often it is visibly linked, because the samples differ.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`); winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (homepage notes):** Résumés are a link. About is a later, shorter block. None of the product-design homepages in that sample open with a paragraph résumé. Dharmesh and Aditya include Resume in the nav.

**Observed (report):** An explicit résumé or CV link in parsed home navigation or content was coded 6/12. The report’s reading: a résumé is useful but is not what makes a portfolio compelling. The checklist still lists “Add résumé/CV” as P1, “a useful hiring conversion layer even though only half the sample exposed one.”

## Disagreement

“Repeated in the nav” (homepage notes) and “6/12” (report) are different samples and different thresholds. The notes did not claim every site shows a résumé. The report did not claim résumés are rare in the nine-site set. Do not merge them into one percentage.

## Implication for this portfolio

Keep a résumé reachable. Do not let it replace the work rows or occupy the intro. The notes suggest the Experience panel is the place for that, if the panel stays.

## Limit

The report does not name which six sites showed the link. Dharmesh’s homepage is described by the report as exposing résumé, email, and five shipped initiatives, so he is at least one of the six.
