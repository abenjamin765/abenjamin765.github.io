# Portfolio metrics were not independently audited

**Claim:** Numbers in these sources are how designers presented results, not figures checked against internal analytics.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`). The homepage notes do not audit metrics.

## Evidence

**Observed (report):** The author evaluated how designers present quantitative evidence and did not have internal analytics to audit conversion, revenue, adoption, or efficiency claims.

**Inferred (report):** The recommendation is to imitate specificity and attribution discipline, not to treat every published number as independently verified.

## Implication for this portfolio

When a work row carries a number, the number needs a baseline, a unit, and a scope this site can defend. Copying another portfolio’s metric style is not a reason to invent a result.

## Limit

The report does not say which two of the twelve sites lacked prominent numeric evidence. Do not guess who they were.
