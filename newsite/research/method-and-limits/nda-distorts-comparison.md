# Confidential work is not a fair visual comparison

**Claim:** A portfolio under a strict NDA can show less and still be a serious sample member.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (report):** The limits section says NDA conditions distort comparison. A designer at a highly confidential company may legitimately expose fewer visuals, artifacts, and metrics than someone on a public product.

**Observed example:** Rahul Jain’s recent work is summarized as scope: leading search and mobile UX for Google Drive, designing Google Meet features, and building Ads reporting systems, with an invitation to reach out for details. Older shareable work supplies additional evidence. The short excerpt the report uses is “Reach out for more details.”

## Implication for this portfolio

If a project cannot be shown, the work row can still name scope, constraints, and what the reader may ask for. Absence of a screenshot is not, by itself, absence of a case.
