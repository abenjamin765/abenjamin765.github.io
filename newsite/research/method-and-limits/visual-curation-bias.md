# The Wall overrepresents designed portfolios

**Claim:** The catalog is likely biased toward people who invest in presentation, because it sorts and markets portfolios by look.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`); supported by the category model in the Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (report):** Wall categorizes portfolios around qualities such as minimalist, dark, interactive, creative, and modern layouts. The report infers that the collection will overrepresent designers who invest materially in portfolio presentation.

**Observed (homepage notes):** The catalog’s primary browse model is look, then role, years, country, company, and “open to work.”

## Implication for this portfolio

A pattern that is common here is a pattern among portfolios already selected for a visual gallery. It is weak evidence that the same pattern is what a hiring loop rewards, and it is a weak reason to pick a theme for this site.

## Limit

The report calls this bias “likely.” It does not measure how many strong portfolios are missing from the Wall.
