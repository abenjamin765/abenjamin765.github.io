# Two samples, not one study

**Claim:** The homepage notes and the winning-portfolios report looked at overlapping Wall of Portfolios sites on the same day, and they are not the same sample.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`); winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (homepage notes):** Nine live homepages on 19 October 2026. September 2026 features: Eemon Roy, Dharmesh Nayak, Aditya Muralidhar, Vaibhav Verma, Rashmi Saini. From the first directory page: Niklas Peterson, Shahriyar Mohammadi, Yinyee Ho, Rishabh Yadav. Homepage text only.

**Observed (report):** Twelve primary sites, coded for structure, cases, visuals, metrics, tools, interaction, accessibility signals, and seniority. The twelve are Michelle Chen, Aditya Muralidhar, Joan Li, Pranshu Agarwal, Eemon Roy, Dharmesh Nayak, Vaibhav Verma, Rahul Jain, Niklas Peterson, Rishabh Yadav, Kat Angeles, and Manolo Beviá. Rashmi is named as a September winner and is not in the twelve. The report read case interiors, not only homepages.

**Overlap:** Eemon Roy, Dharmesh Nayak, Aditya Muralidhar, Vaibhav Verma, Niklas Peterson, Rishabh Yadav.

**Only in the homepage notes:** Rashmi Saini, Shahriyar Mohammadi, Yinyee Ho.

**Only in the report:** Michelle Chen, Joan Li, Pranshu Agarwal, Rahul Jain, Kat Angeles, Manolo Beviá.

**Observed (homepage notes only):** Amazon’s company page on the Wall was a thin list of names and added no homepage evidence. The `?company=All` query did not return a company-filtered list.

## Disagreement

Do not average these into one “12 + 9” study. Counts such as 10/12 or “all nine homepages” belong to the sample that produced them.

## Implication for this portfolio

Use a pattern for the intro, work, about, or contact panels only when the file says which sample it came from. A pattern from case interiors is not evidence about the first screen.

## Limit

Neither sample is random, and neither is a set of US staff or principal product designers. The homepage notes say the September features are mostly product designers with about six years of experience, several in India and on consumer products. The report says the four product-oriented September winners are concentrated in India/UAE, then adds eight accepted portfolios for broader role and geographic coverage. Both call the selection purposive.
