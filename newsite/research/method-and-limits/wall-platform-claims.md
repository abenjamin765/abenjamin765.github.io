# Wall platform claims are not audited rates

**Claim:** The Wall’s own headlines, reach line, category counts, and acceptance line are printed claims, not verified market facts.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`); winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed (homepage notes):** On 19 October 2026 the homepage headline was “Discover work from the world’s top designers.” Under it, a logo row and the claim “Trusted by 50,000+ designers from” Netflix, Google, Apple, Uber, Swiggy, CRED, and Microsoft. Category counts printed on the page: Minimalist 850+, Dark themed 400+, Interactive 320+, Creative 200+, Modern layout 90+. A welcome overlay on profile pages says “Only 30% of portfolios make it to the Wall.”

**Observed (report):** The same 30% line is described as a statement on individual profile pages. Category counts are given as more than 850 minimalist, 400 dark-themed, 320 interactive, 200 creative, and 90 modern-layout portfolios. The report says those categories overlap, so they are not a denominator for statistics.

**Inferred (both):** “50,000+,” “world’s top,” and “30%” are source claims. The homepage notes say they are not verified reach or an audited acceptance rate. The report says the 30% figure is the Wall’s own claim rather than an independently audited selection rate.

## Implication for this portfolio

Do not cite 30% or 50,000+ on this site as if they were hiring statistics. They explain how the catalog markets itself.

## Limit

The two write-ups of the category counts differ slightly (“850+” versus “more than 850”). Neither source publishes the Wall’s counting method.
