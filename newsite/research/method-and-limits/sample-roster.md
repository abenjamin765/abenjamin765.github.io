**Observed mismatch (homepage notes):** Directory tags and live copy sometimes disagree. Niklas is tagged Dark Theme while the homepage is about pixels, code, and case studies. Shahi is tagged Minimal and filed as senior, while the site says staff product designer at Deel. The notes say to treat the live page as the design evidence and the wall tag as a catalog label.

## Implication for this portfolio

Quote a designer when the pattern needs a witness. Do not add a designer, a metric, or a title that is not in the table above or in the file you are using.

## Limit

The report says seniority labels are not standardized and that actual responsibilities are more informative than title alone. The years and titles above are how the sources labeled people, not a leveling system for this site.
