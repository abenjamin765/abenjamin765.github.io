# Positioning, then evidence, then decisions

**Claim:** The report’s central finding is an information order, not a visual template, a project count, or a required case length.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Inferred (report):** What was consistent is this hierarchy: positioning, then selected evidence, then consequential decisions, then visible product or craft, then outcome, then credibility, then contact.

**Inferred (report):** The practical definition offered is not “beautiful case studies.” It is “a compressed evidence system for the next level of responsibility.”

## Disagreement

The homepage notes never state that full chain, because they did not read case interiors. Their homepage version is shorter: who it is and the current or most recent role, one claim about the kind of designer, then the work. See `homepage-information-order.md`.

The phrase “next level of responsibility” is the report’s framing. The homepage notes do not establish what Aaron should be hired to own next.

## Implication for this portfolio

Intro carries positioning. Work carries selected evidence. Decisions, craft, and outcome belong in the work story, not in a second hero. Contact stays easy to find. Credibility (about, résumé, testimony) is not the first screen in either source.

## Limit

“Remarkably consistent” is the report’s judgment of 12 purposive sites. It is not a rate for all UX portfolios.
