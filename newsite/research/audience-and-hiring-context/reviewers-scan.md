# Reviewers are described as scanning

**Claim:** The report borrows the idea that hiring managers are time constrained and often scan, so the page should yield an argument without a long read.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`).

## Evidence

**Borrowed:** Nielsen Norman Group, as summarized by the report: hiring managers are time constrained; portfolio material benefits from curation rather than extensive prose; hiring managers often scan rather than read extensive prose.

**Inferred (report):** The strongest portfolios optimize for information scent before immersion. A recruiter does not need to decipher an artistic concept to discover what someone does. Role and level appear immediately, work is exposed quickly, and project cards say something substantive about the problem or consequence.

## Implication for this portfolio

Intro and work rows should be readable in a glance. That does not specify whether the newsite panels scroll or snap. The homepage notes say that interaction choice is still open.

## Limit

No timing data and no hiring-manager session is in either source. “Unusually quickly” and “scan” are borrowed or inferred, not measured on these sites.
