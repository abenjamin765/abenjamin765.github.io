# The question these notes were written to answer

**Claim:** The homepage notes exist to decide what the first screen says, and how the rest of the site introduces work, so a design director can tell quickly what kind of product designer this is.

**Sources:** Wall of Portfolios notes (`newsite/research.md`).

## Evidence

**Observed:** That decision sentence is the stated purpose of the homepage notes. Three questions follow: what the Wall treats as worth featuring; what those designers put in the hero, navigation, and work list; which patterns fit a staff-level product portfolio about complex, shipped work, and which belong to a different kind of site.

**Inferred (homepage notes, about the current production site):** The notes say the production hero already claims to help teams make sense of complex problems, then ship something people can use. That sentence is attributed to the portfolio in `src/index.pug`. It was not re-checked while building this library.

## Implication for this portfolio

The newsite panels in `newsite/index.html` are Intro, Experience, Work, About, and Contact. The research question maps most directly onto Intro and Work. Experience, About, and Contact are in the notes only where navigation and résumé come up.

## Limit

“Design director” and “staff-level product portfolio” are the audience and altitude named in the homepage notes. They are not a measured hiring target, and the HTML title is “Aaron Benjamin - Software Designer,” which neither source defines.
