# The interview is a different interface

**Claim:** The report says the portfolio site should not be the interview presentation.

**Sources:** Winning-portfolios report (attachment `deep-research-report__10_.md`). Not in the homepage notes.

## Evidence

**Borrowed:** Nielsen Norman Group, as the report summarizes it, recommends building a presentation from the best case instead of scrolling the site during the interview, rehearsing, and using structured behavioral storytelling such as STAR. Interview guidance also includes structured examples of compromise, stakeholder issues, and usability-testing decisions.

**Inferred (report):** The web portfolio and the interview are different interfaces. The checklist puts “prepare interview deck” and “run mock interviews” at P2, after the site’s evidence path works. Validation questions for a homepage critique are: What level do you think I am? What kind of problems do I solve? Which project would you open? What evidence do you remember? What is my strongest capability?

## Implication for this portfolio

Nothing in the intro, experience, work, about, or contact panels needs to become a slide deck. This file is about a later artifact. It should not add interview structure to the homepage.

## Limit

STAR and the presentation advice are borrowed. The report does not show that the sampled designers used them.
