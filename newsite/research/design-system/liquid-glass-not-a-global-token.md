# Liquid Glass is not a global surface token

**Claim:** The report says Liquid Glass is a native Apple treatment for the control layer, not a material to apply to every surface or to recreate on other platforms.

**Sources:** Metro × Apple design-system report (attachment `deep-research-report__11_.md`).

## Evidence

**Borrowed:** The report says Apple introduced Liquid Glass and rebuilt system UI around it in 2025, and that 2026 guidance continues that direction, including stronger readability and separation and user-adjustable transparency or tint. Apps that use standard system structures, as the report summarizes 2026 SwiftUI guidance, pick up revised glass behavior automatically.

**Prescribed:** Do not put glass on flat content for style. Do not imitate Liquid Glass with custom drawing or with translucent cards on other platforms. Do not add `liquidGlass` to a shared product API. Web and Windows resolve the same semantic surface another way.

## Implication for this portfolio

This site has no Apple control layer. The portable rule is the negative one: do not frost the work rows or the intro to look like system glass.
